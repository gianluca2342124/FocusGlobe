## Description (draft)
FocusGlobe turns a focus timer into a calm balloon journey.

Instead of staring at a countdown, you choose a beautiful route and take off.
A soft balloon drifts across a real map while you study, write, code or do deep
work — and you land the moment your focus session is complete.

• Routes from 5 minutes to 12 hours — micro sessions to ultra deep work
• A calm, map-first experience designed to keep you in flow
• Pure Mode hides everything but the map, the balloon and the time
• Earn Focus Miles, build a streak, and collect postcards in your Globe Passport
• Beautiful in Light and Dark mode
• Your focus history stays on your device — no account, no location, no tracking

Leave distractions below. Stay focused until you land.

## Notes for review
- v1 requests **no** permissions (no location, no notifications, no tracking).
- All focus data is stored locally on device.
- Ads and purchases are **mocked** in this build (no real network ad/IAP).
- The map renders via Google Maps SDK for iOS (display only — no Directions,
  Routes, Places, Geocoding or Search APIs are used). Routes are curated locally.
